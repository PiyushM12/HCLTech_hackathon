# Amendment: Revised Income Limit for Vriddhjan Pension Yojana
Source: CIR-2026-004 | Issued: 2026-06-15 | Effective from: 2026-07-01

Issued by: Department of Social Welfare, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Amendment No.: SW/PEN/2026/077 | Date of issue: 2026-06-15 (15 June 2026) | Scheme: Vriddhjan Pension Yojana (SCH-PEN-01)
Amends: Section 3 (Eligibility) of GL-PEN-01

## 1 Background
Section 3 of GL-PEN-01 allows the pension only when the annual household income is ₹1,20,000 or less.
Many older persons with small family incomes were left out because of this limit.
The state government has decided to raise the limit.

## 2 Revised income limit
The annual household income limit in Section 3 of GL-PEN-01 is raised from ₹1,20,000 to ₹1,50,000 with effect from 2026-07-01 (1 July 2026).
From that date, a person aged 60 or more whose annual household income is ₹1,50,000 or less can get the pension, if the other conditions are met.
The meaning of household income does not change. It is still the total yearly income of all members who live together.
All other conditions in Section 3 of GL-PEN-01 stay the same.

## 3 Effective date
This amendment takes effect from 2026-07-01 (1 July 2026).
Applications decided before 2026-07-01 are not reopened automatically.
A person whose application was rejected only because the household income was above ₹1,20,000 may apply again on or after 2026-07-01.

## 4 Instructions for service centres
From 2026-07-01 (1 July 2026), use the limit of ₹1,50,000 for all new pension applications.
Do not turn away an applicant whose household income is between ₹1,20,001 and ₹1,50,000.
