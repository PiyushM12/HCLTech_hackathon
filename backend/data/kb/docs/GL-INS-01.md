# Suraksha Bima Yojana – Scheme Guidelines 2026-27
Source: GL-INS-01 | Issued: 2026-08-25 | Effective from: 2026-09-01

Issued by: Department of Labour, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: LAB/INS/2026/063 | Date of issue: 2026-08-25 (25 August 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-INS-01

## 1 Purpose
Suraksha Bima Yojana (SCH-INS-01) gives free accident insurance to workers who earn a daily or seasonal living.
It protects the family if the worker dies or is disabled in an accident.
These guidelines apply to the 2026-27 enrolment from 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026).

## 2 Benefit
An enrolled worker gets accident insurance cover of ₹2,00,000.
The yearly premium is paid by the state. The worker pays nothing.
If the worker dies in an accident, the nominee receives the claim amount.

## 3 Eligibility
A worker can enrol only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The worker is at least 18 years and not more than 59 years of age on the date of enrolment.

3.2 The worker's occupation is one of these: daily wage worker, farmer, construction worker or street vendor.

3.3 The annual household income is ₹2,50,000 or less.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Age proof certificate | Mandatory | Any government document showing date of birth |
| Occupation certificate or labour card | Mandatory | Labour card or a letter from the Gram Panchayat |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |
| Nominee details form | Optional | Can be filled at the service centre |

## 5 How to apply
1. Visit any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent) during the enrolment window.
2. The operator fills in the enrolment form online. There is no fee and no premium to pay.
3. Give the name of your nominee.
4. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number. A policy card is sent after approval.

## 6 Last date
The enrolment window is from 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026) to 2026-11-20 (20 November 2026).
Enrolment is not possible outside this window.

## 7 Decision
The Department of Labour checks each enrolment and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an enrolment is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the worker can file a grievance.
