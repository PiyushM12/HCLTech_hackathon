# Shahari Kiraya Sahayata Yojana – Scheme Guidelines
Source: GL-HSG-02 | Issued: 2026-11-05 | Effective from: 2027-01-15

Issued by: Department of Urban Development, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: UD/SKS/2026/015 | Date of issue: 2026-11-05 (5 November 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-HSG-02

## 1 Purpose
Shahari Kiraya Sahayata Yojana (SCH-HSG-02) helps low-income families in towns and cities pay their house rent.
These guidelines come into force on 2027-01-15 (15 January 2027). No application can be made before that date.

## 2 Benefit
An approved household gets ₹3,000 per month towards house rent, for up to 12 months.
The money is paid directly into the applicant's bank account.

## 3 Eligibility
A person can get the benefit only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The person lives in an urban area (a town or city under a municipal body).

3.2 The annual household income is ₹1,50,000 or less.

3.3 The applicant is 18 years of age or more.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rent agreement | Mandatory | Registered or notarised |
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued in the last 12 months |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |

## 5 How to apply
1. From 2027-01-15 (15 January 2027), visit any service centre (Nagar Seva Kendra or equivalent) with your documents.
2. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
3. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.

## 6 Last date
Applications will be accepted from 2027-01-15 (15 January 2027) to 2027-03-31 (31 March 2027).

## 7 Decision
The Department of Urban Development checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the applicant can file a grievance.
