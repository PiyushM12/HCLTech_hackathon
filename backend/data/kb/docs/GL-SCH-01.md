# Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti – Scheme Guidelines 2026-27
Source: GL-SCH-01 | Issued: 2026-08-20 | Effective from: 2026-09-01

Issued by: Department of School and Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: SHE/KVC/2026/118 | Date of issue: 2026-08-20 (20 August 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-SCH-01

## 1 Purpose
Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01) helps girls continue their studies from Class 9 up to graduation.
It supports families with a low household income so that daughters do not leave school or college for lack of money.
These guidelines apply to the 2026-27 academic year from 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026).

## 2 Benefit
An approved student gets a scholarship of ₹12,000 per year.
The money is paid directly into the student's bank account (or a joint account with a parent).
The student must apply again in each new academic year.

## 3 Eligibility
A student can get the scholarship only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The applicant is a girl student.

3.2 She is studying in any class from Class 9 up to graduation in a recognised school or college.

3.3 She is at least 14 years and not more than 25 years of age on the date of application.

3.4 The annual household income is ₹2,50,000 or less.

What "household income" means: the total yearly income of all members who live together in the same house, including parents and brothers or sisters who earn.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| School or college enrolment certificate | Mandatory | For the current academic year |
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued in the last 12 months |
| Previous year marksheet | Mandatory | Class 8 or later |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | Account in the student's name or joint with a parent |
| Residence certificate | Optional | Needed only if the school is outside Mitrapur Pradesh |

## 5 How to apply
1. The student, with a parent or guardian, visits any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent).
2. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
3. The school or college head confirms the enrolment online after the form is submitted.
4. The student gets an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.

## 6 Last date
Applications for the 2026-27 cycle open on 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026).
The last date for applications is 2026-11-30 (30 November 2026).
Applications received after the last date will not be accepted.

## 7 Decision
The Department of School and Higher Education checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the family can file a grievance.
