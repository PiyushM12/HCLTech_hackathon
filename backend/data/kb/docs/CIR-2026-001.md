# Circular: Monthly Pension Credit Date
Source: CIR-2026-001 | Issued: 2026-04-20 | Effective from: 2026-05-01

Issued by: Department of Social Welfare, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Circular No.: SW/PEN/2026/058 | Date of issue: 2026-04-20 (20 April 2026) | Scheme: Vriddhjan Pension Yojana (SCH-PEN-01)

## 1 Background
Many pensioners asked on which day the monthly pension of ₹1,500 under Vriddhjan Pension Yojana (SCH-PEN-01) reaches their bank account.
Section 2 of GL-PEN-01 says the pension is paid every month, but does not fix a date.

## 2 Credit date
From 2026-05-01 (1 May 2026), the monthly pension will be credited to the pensioner's bank account by the 10th of each month.
Example: the pension for May 2026 will be credited by 2026-05-10 (10 May 2026).
If the 10th is a bank holiday, the money may reach the account on the next working day.

## 3 Effective date
This circular takes effect from 2026-05-01 (1 May 2026).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
If a pensioner says the money has not arrived by the 10th, check the payment status with the acknowledgement or beneficiary number.
If the payment failed (for example, a closed bank account), help the pensioner update the bank details. This is free.
