# Circular: Opening Date for Shahari Kiraya Sahayata Yojana
Source: CIR-2026-014 | Issued: 2026-11-05 | Effective from: 2027-01-15

Issued by: Department of Urban Development, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Circular No.: UD/SKS/2026/016 | Date of issue: 2026-11-05 (5 November 2026) | Scheme: Shahari Kiraya Sahayata Yojana (SCH-HSG-02)

## 1 Background
The state government has approved Shahari Kiraya Sahayata Yojana (SCH-HSG-02) to help low-income urban families pay house rent. The rules are in GL-HSG-02.

## 2 Opening date
Shahari Kiraya Sahayata Yojana applications will open on 2027-01-15 (15 January 2027).
The last date for applications is 2027-03-31 (31 March 2027).
No application can be accepted before 2027-01-15. Nobody can "pre-register" you for this scheme.

## 3 Effective date
This circular takes effect from 2027-01-15 (15 January 2027).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
Until 2027-01-15, tell citizens that the scheme has not opened yet and which documents to keep ready: a registered or notarised rent agreement, an income certificate issued in the last 12 months, and a bank passbook.
