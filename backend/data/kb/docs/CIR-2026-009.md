# Circular: Closure of Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana
Source: CIR-2026-009 | Issued: 2026-09-25 | Effective from: 2026-10-01

Issued by: Department of Agriculture, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Circular No.: AGR/KYA/2026/088 | Date of issue: 2026-09-25 (25 September 2026) | Scheme: Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana (SCH-FRM-02)

## 1 Background
Section 6 of GL-FRM-02 fixed the last date for applications under Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana (SCH-FRM-02) as 2026-09-30 (30 September 2026).
The budget for 2026-27 has been fully committed.

## 2 Closure of the scheme
Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana (SCH-FRM-02) is closed for new applications with effect from 2026-10-01 (1 October 2026).
No new application will be accepted on or after 2026-10-01.
Applications received by 2026-09-30 (30 September 2026) will still be processed and decided as per GL-FRM-02.

## 3 Effective date
This circular takes effect from 2026-10-01 (1 October 2026).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
Do not accept new applications under SCH-FRM-02 from 2026-10-01.
Farmers who ask about machinery support should be told that the scheme is closed for new applications. Any future scheme will be announced by the Department of Agriculture.
Farmers who applied by 2026-09-30 can check their status with their acknowledgement number.
