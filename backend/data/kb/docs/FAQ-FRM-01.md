# Kisan Samriddhi Yojana FAQ (Hinglish)
Source: FAQ-FRM-01 | Issued: 2026-08-12 | Effective from: 2026-08-12

Prepared by: Department of Agriculture (Krishi Vibhag), Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Reference No.: AGR/FAQ/2026/004 | Date: 2026-08-12 (12 August 2026) | Scheme: Kisan Samriddhi Yojana (SCH-FRM-01)
Yeh FAQ aam madad ke liye hai. Official niyam GL-FRM-01 aur uske circulars mein hain.

## 1 Kisan Samriddhi Yojana mein kitna paisa milta hai?
Har saal ₹6,000 milta hai, ₹2,000 ki teen kiston mein: April, August aur December mein.
Paisa seedha aapke bank khate mein aata hai (Direct Bank Transfer). Cash kabhi nahi milta.

## 2 Kaun apply kar sakta hai?
Jo vyakti kheti karta hai (farmer), wahi apply kar sakta hai.
Zameen kam se kam 0.5 acre aur zyada se zyada 5 acre honi chahiye.
Ghar ki saalana aay (household income) ₹3,00,000 ya usse kam honi chahiye.

## 3 Land limit 4 acre thi na? Ab 5 acre kaise?
Haan, pehle limit 4 acre thi. Circular CIR-2026-007 ke baad 2026-08-01 (1 August 2026) se maximum land limit 4 acre se badhkar 5 acre ho gayi hai.
Minimum 0.5 acre wahi hai. Agar aapke paas 4.5 acre zameen hai aur pehle mana ho gaya tha, toh ab dobara apply kar sakte ho.

## 4 Main bataidar / kiraye ka kisan hoon. Kya main apply kar sakta hoon?
Haan. Agar aapke paas tenancy agreement (batai / kirayedari anubandh) hai, toh aap bhi farmer maane jaoge.
Tenancy agreement khatauni (land record) ki jagah accept hota hai. Agreement mein jitni zameen likhi hai, wahi gini jaati hai.

## 5 Kaun se documents chahiye?
- Land record (khatauni) — zaroori. Current record jisme aapka naam aur area ho.
- Income certificate — zaroori. Pichhle 12 mahine mein bana ho.
- Bank passbook — zaroori. Pehla page jisme account holder ka naam ho.
- Tenancy agreement — sirf bataidar kisan ke liye; khatauni ki jagah chalega.

## 6 Kist kab aati hai?
April, August aur December mein ek-ek kist ₹2,000 ki aati hai.
Agar aapka application kist ke mahine se pehle approve ho gaya, toh us mahine ki kist milegi.
Decision ka target application ki date se 45 din hai. Agar 45 din ke baad bhi kuch nahi hua, toh grievance file karo.

## 7 Koi fees lagti hai?
Bilkul nahi. Apply karna free hai (POL-FEE-01). Koi "release fee" nahi hoti.
Koi phone pe OTP ya PIN maange toh mat dena, fraud desk ko batao.

## 8 Apply kahan karein? Last date kya hai?
Kisi bhi service centre (Jan Seva Kendra) pe jaake apply karo. Koi last date nahi hai, saal bhar apply kar sakte ho.
Apply karne ke baad acknowledgement slip milegi — uska number sambhal ke rakho, status check karne ke kaam aayega.
