# Policy on Fees and Fraud Protection
Source: POL-FEE-01 | Issued: 2026-04-01 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Public Services and e-Governance, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: PSG/POL/2026/001 | Date of issue: 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026) | Applies to: all schemes

## 1 Purpose
This policy tells citizens what they never have to pay for, and how to stay safe from people who pretend to be officials.
It applies to every welfare scheme of Mitrapur Pradesh and to every service centre from 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026).

## 2 No fee for applications
There is no fee for any scheme application.
There is no fee for help at a service centre. Filling the form, scanning documents, printing the acknowledgement slip and checking your status are all free.
There is no "release fee", "processing fee" or "approval fee" in any scheme. Anyone who asks for such a fee is not following the rules.
Scheme money is paid only by direct bank transfer into the beneficiary's own bank account. No official will bring cash to your house or ask you to pay first to "release" your money.

## 3 Never share OTP or bank PIN
No government official, service centre operator or the SchemeSathi assistant will ever ask for your OTP, bank PIN, ATM PIN or internet banking password.
Do not share these with anyone, on a phone call, SMS, WhatsApp or in person.
If someone asks for them, it is a fraud. End the call.

## 4 Report fraud
If someone asks you for money, an OTP or a PIN in the name of a scheme, report it to the fraud desk.
You can reach the fraud desk through any service centre or through the SchemeSathi assistant.
Give the phone number that called you, the date and time, and what was said. Do not pay any money.
If you have already shared your OTP or PIN, call your bank at once to block your account or card, then report to the fraud desk.
