# Policy on Service Centre Appointments
Source: POL-APPT-01 | Issued: 2026-04-01 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Public Services and e-Governance, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: PSG/POL/2026/002 | Date of issue: 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026) | Applies to: all schemes

## 1 Purpose
Citizens can book a visit to a service centre so that they do not have to wait in a queue.
This policy sets the rules for booking. Walk-in visits are still allowed during working hours.

## 2 Booking lead time
A visit must be booked at least 24 hours before the visit time.
Example: to visit at 11:00 on Tuesday, book by 11:00 on Monday.

## 3 Booking window
A visit can be booked at most 30 days ahead.
Visits can only be booked on the centre's working days and within its working hours.

## 4 Confirmation window
When the SchemeSathi assistant or an operator proposes a booking (or any other action), the citizen must confirm it within 30 minutes.
If it is not confirmed within 30 minutes, the proposal expires and nothing is booked. The citizen can ask again.

## 5 Reminders
A reminder is sent at 18:00 (6 pm) on the day before the visit.
The reminder gives the centre name, address, time and the documents to bring.
