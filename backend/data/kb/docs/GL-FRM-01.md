# Kisan Samriddhi Yojana – Scheme Guidelines
Source: GL-FRM-01 | Issued: 2026-03-25 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Agriculture, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: AGR/KSY/2026/022 | Date of issue: 2026-03-25 (25 March 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-FRM-01

## 1 Purpose
Kisan Samriddhi Yojana (SCH-FRM-01) gives yearly income support to small and marginal farmers in Mitrapur Pradesh.
The money helps farmers buy seeds, fertiliser and other inputs on time.
These guidelines apply from 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026).

## 2 Benefit
An approved farmer gets ₹6,000 per year.
It is paid in three instalments of ₹2,000 each:
- the first instalment in April,
- the second instalment in August,
- the third instalment in December.

The money is paid directly into the farmer's bank account.

## 3 Eligibility
A person can get the benefit only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The person's occupation is farming. A tenant farmer who has a tenancy agreement for the land is also treated as a farmer under this scheme.

3.2 The land held by the farmer is at least 0.5 acre and not more than 4 acres. For a tenant farmer, the land in the tenancy agreement is counted.

3.3 The annual household income is ₹3,00,000 or less.

What "household income" means: the total yearly income of all members who live together in the same house, from farming and from all other sources.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Land record (khatauni) | Mandatory | Current record showing the farmer's name and area |
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued in the last 12 months |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |
| Tenancy agreement | Optional | Only for tenant farmers; accepted in place of the land record |

## 5 How to apply
1. Visit any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent) with your documents.
2. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
3. The patwari or village accountant verifies the land details online.
4. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.

## 6 Last date
There is no last date. Applications are open throughout the year.
A farmer approved before an instalment month gets that instalment.

## 7 Decision
The Department of Agriculture checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the farmer can file a grievance.
