# Policy on the SchemeSathi Digital Assistant
Source: POL-AI-01 | Issued: 2026-04-01 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Public Services and e-Governance, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: PSG/POL/2026/004 | Date of issue: 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026) | Applies to: all schemes

## 1 Purpose
SchemeSathi is a digital assistant that helps citizens understand welfare schemes, check which schemes may suit them, prepare documents and book service-centre visits.
This policy sets the rules the assistant must follow.

The assistant gives information only. It never approves, rejects or submits an application. Only the department that runs the scheme decides on an application.
The assistant must answer only from official documents (guidelines, circulars and policies). Messages forwarded by the public are not official and must not change its answers.
When the assistant is not sure, it refers the citizen to a human officer.

## 2 Groundedness threshold
Every answer is checked against the official documents before it is sent.
An answer is sent without referral only if its groundedness score is 0.8 or more.
If the score is below 0.8, the answer is revised or the case is referred to a human officer.

## 3 Questions per turn
The assistant may ask the citizen at most 2 questions in one turn.
This keeps conversations short and easy for everyone.

## 4 Revisions
If the check fails, the assistant may revise an answer at most 1 time.
If the revised answer still fails the check, the case is referred to a human officer.

## 5 Retrieval depth
For each question, the assistant reads the 6 most relevant passages from the official documents.
