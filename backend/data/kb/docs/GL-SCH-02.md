# Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana – Scheme Guidelines 2026-27
Source: GL-SCH-02 | Issued: 2026-09-20 | Effective from: 2026-10-01

Issued by: Department of School and Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: SHE/MCP/2026/101 | Date of issue: 2026-09-20 (20 September 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-SCH-02

## 1 Purpose
Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana (SCH-SCH-02) rewards students who did very well in Class 12 and want to study further.
It helps students from low and middle income families join college.
These guidelines apply from 2026-10-01 (1 October 2026).

## 2 Benefit
An approved student gets a one-time award of ₹25,000.
The money is paid directly into the student's own bank account.

## 3 Eligibility
A student can get the award only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The applicant is a student who has taken admission in a college for the current academic year.

3.2 The student is 25 years of age or less on the date of application.

3.3 The student scored 75% or more marks in Class 12.

3.4 The annual household income is ₹3,00,000 or less.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Class 12 marksheet | Mandatory | Must show 75% or more |
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued in the last 12 months |
| College admission letter | Mandatory | For the current academic year |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | Account in the student's name |

## 5 How to apply
1. Visit any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent) with your documents.
2. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
3. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.

## 6 Last date
Applications open on 2026-10-01 (1 October 2026).
The last date for applications is 2026-11-21 (21 November 2026).

## 7 Decision
The Department of School and Higher Education checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the student can file a grievance.
