# Amendment: Last Date Extended for Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana
Source: CIR-2026-020 | Issued: 2026-11-21 | Effective from: 2026-11-21

Issued by: Department of School and Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Amendment No.: SHE/MCP/2026/131 | Date of issue: 2026-11-21 (21 November 2026) | Scheme: Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana (SCH-SCH-02)
Amends: Section 6 (Last date) of GL-SCH-02 | Supersedes: CIR-2026-015

## 1 Background
Section 6 of GL-SCH-02 and circular CIR-2026-015 fixed the last date for applications under Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana (SCH-SCH-02) as 2026-11-21 (21 November 2026).
Many colleges have not yet issued admission letters for students admitted in the last round of counselling.

## 2 Extension of last date
The last date for applications under Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana (SCH-SCH-02) is extended from 2026-11-21 (21 November 2026) to 2026-12-05 (5 December 2026).
This amends Section 6 of GL-SCH-02.
This amendment supersedes CIR-2026-015, which said there would be no extension.
All eligibility rules and documents stay the same. The income limit for applications made before 2027-01-01 (1 January 2027) remains ₹3,00,000.

## 3 Effective date
This amendment takes effect from 2026-11-21 (21 November 2026).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
Accept applications under SCH-SCH-02 up to and including 2026-12-05 (5 December 2026).
Tell students who were told the last date is 2026-11-21 that they now have time until 2026-12-05.
