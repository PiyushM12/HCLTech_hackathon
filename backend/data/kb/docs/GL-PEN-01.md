# Vriddhjan Pension Yojana – Scheme Guidelines
Source: GL-PEN-01 | Issued: 2026-03-20 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Social Welfare, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: SW/PEN/2026/041 | Date of issue: 2026-03-20 (20 March 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-PEN-01

## 1 Purpose
Vriddhjan Pension Yojana (SCH-PEN-01) gives a monthly pension to older persons in Mitrapur Pradesh who have a low household income.
The aim is to help them meet basic daily needs with dignity.
These guidelines apply from 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026) in all four districts: Nayagaon, Sundarpur, Kaverinadu and Thenpuram.

## 2 Benefit
An approved applicant gets ₹1,500 per month.
The money is paid directly into the applicant's own bank account.
No cash is paid at any office.
The pension continues every month as long as the person remains eligible.

## 3 Eligibility
A person can get the pension only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The person is 60 years of age or more on the date of application.

3.2 The annual household income is ₹1,20,000 or less.

3.3 The person lives in Mitrapur Pradesh.

What "household income" means: household income is the total yearly income of all members who live together in the same house and share the same kitchen. It includes wages, farm income, business income, pensions and rent received by any of these members.

Example: a 64-year-old woman lives with her son and daughter-in-law. The son earns ₹90,000 a year and the daughter-in-law earns ₹20,000 a year. The household income is ₹1,10,000. This is within the limit.

## 4 Documents
Bring the original and one photocopy of each document.

| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Age proof certificate | Mandatory | Birth certificate, school leaving certificate or a certificate from a government doctor |
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued by the Tehsildar in the last 12 months |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |
| Residence certificate | Mandatory | Or ration card showing a Mitrapur address |
| Passport-size photograph | Optional | Two copies; can be taken at the service centre |

## 5 How to apply
1. Go to any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra, Nagar Seva Kendra, Nagarika Seva Kendra or Makkal Seva Maiyam) in your district.
2. The operator fills in the application form online with you. Applying is free of cost.
3. The operator scans your documents and returns the originals to you.
4. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number. Keep it safe. You need it to check your status.

A family member can come with you to help. Only the applicant can sign or give a thumb impression.

## 6 Last date
There is no last date. Applications are open throughout the year.

## 7 Decision
The Department of Social Welfare checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
The decision is sent by SMS and can also be checked at the service centre.
No service centre operator, agent or assistant can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing. The applicant can file a grievance.
