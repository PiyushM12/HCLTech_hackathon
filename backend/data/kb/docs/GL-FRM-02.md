# Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana – Scheme Guidelines 2026-27
Source: GL-FRM-02 | Issued: 2026-03-25 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Agriculture, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: AGR/KYA/2026/023 | Date of issue: 2026-03-25 (25 March 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-FRM-02

## 1 Purpose
Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana (SCH-FRM-02) helps farmers buy approved farm machinery such as power tillers, seed drills and sprayers.
These guidelines apply from 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026).

## 2 Benefit
An approved farmer gets a subsidy of 50% of the price of approved farm machinery, up to a maximum of ₹50,000.
The subsidy is paid directly into the farmer's bank account after the machine is bought from an approved dealer and checked.

## 3 Eligibility
A person can get the subsidy only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The person's occupation is farming.

3.2 The land held by the farmer is 10 acres or less.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Land record (khatauni) | Mandatory | Current record |
| Quotation from an approved dealer | Mandatory | Dealer must be on the department list |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |

## 5 How to apply
1. Get a quotation from a dealer on the department's approved list.
2. Visit any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent) with your documents.
3. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
4. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.
5. Buy the machine only after you receive the approval letter.

## 6 Last date
Applications open on 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026).
The last date for applications is 2026-09-30 (30 September 2026).

## 7 Decision
The Department of Agriculture checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the farmer can file a grievance.
