# Amendment: Revised Income Limit for Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana
Source: CIR-2026-013 | Issued: 2026-10-30 | Effective from: 2027-01-01

Issued by: Department of School and Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Amendment No.: SHE/MCP/2026/117 | Date of issue: 2026-10-30 (30 October 2026) | Scheme: Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana (SCH-SCH-02)
Amends: Section 3 (Eligibility) of GL-SCH-02

## 1 Background
Section 3 of GL-SCH-02 allows the award only when the annual household income is ₹3,00,000 or less.
The state government has decided to include more students from middle-income families from the next calendar year.

## 2 Revised income limit
The annual household income limit in Section 3 of GL-SCH-02 is raised from ₹3,00,000 to ₹4,00,000 for applications made on or after 2027-01-01 (1 January 2027).
Applications made before 2027-01-01 are still decided with the limit of ₹3,00,000.
All other conditions in Section 3 of GL-SCH-02 stay the same, including 75% or more marks in Class 12 and age 25 or less.

## 3 Effective date
This amendment takes effect from 2027-01-01 (1 January 2027). It is not in force before that date.

## 4 Instructions for service centres
Until 2026-12-31 (31 December 2026), use the limit of ₹3,00,000.
From 2027-01-01 (1 January 2027), use the limit of ₹4,00,000 for new applications, whenever an application window is open.
