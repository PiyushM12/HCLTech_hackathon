# How to Apply at a Service Centre – Process Note
Source: PRC-APPLY-01 | Issued: 2026-04-01 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Public Services and e-Governance, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Reference No.: PSG/PRC/2026/001 | Date of issue: 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026) | Applies to: all schemes

## 1 Before you go
Check which scheme you want to apply for and read its list of documents.
Keep the originals and one photocopy of each document ready.
You can walk in during working hours, or book a visit through the SchemeSathi assistant. A booking must be made at least 24 hours ahead.

## 2 At the service centre
Go to your nearest service centre: Jan Seva Kendra or Nagar Seva Kendra in Nayagaon and Sundarpur, Nagarika Seva Kendra in Kaverinadu, and Makkal Seva Maiyam in Thenpuram.
Tell the operator which scheme you want to apply for.
The operator fills in the form online with you and scans your documents. The originals are returned to you.
This service is free. Do not pay anyone.

## 3 Signing the application
Check the details on the screen before you sign: your name, age, bank account number and income.
The applicant signs or gives a thumb impression. Nobody else can sign for an adult applicant.

## 4 Acknowledgement number
After the form is submitted, you get an acknowledgement slip.
The slip has an acknowledgement number. Keep it safe. You need it to check your status or to file a grievance.

## 5 Decision
The department that runs the scheme checks the application and decides on it. The service centre does not decide.
The target is a decision within 45 days of the date of application.
You get an SMS when a decision is made. You can also check the status at the service centre or through the SchemeSathi assistant.

## 6 If your application is rejected
The reason for rejection is given in writing.
If the reason is a mistake in a document (for example, a wrong age or a wrong bank account number), get the document corrected and apply again.
If you think the decision is wrong, file a grievance at any service centre or through the SchemeSathi assistant. It is free.
A grievance must be resolved within 30 working days.

## 7 If there is a delay
If there is no decision after 45 days, you can file a grievance about the delay.
Give your acknowledgement number when you file it.
