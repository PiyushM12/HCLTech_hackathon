# Frequently Asked Questions – Schemes and Applications
Source: FAQ-GEN-01 | Issued: 2026-06-01 | Effective from: 2026-06-01

Prepared by: Department of Public Services and e-Governance, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Reference No.: PSG/FAQ/2026/001 | Date: 2026-06-01 (1 June 2026)
These answers are for general help. The scheme guidelines and circulars are the official rules.

## 1 What is an income certificate?
An income certificate is an official paper that shows the yearly income of your household.
It is issued by the Tehsildar's office. You can apply for it at any service centre.
For all schemes, an income certificate is valid for 12 months from its date of issue (circular CIR-2026-002). If yours is older, get a new one before you apply.

## 2 What is household income?
Household income is the total yearly income of all members who live together in the same house and share the same kitchen.
It includes wages, farm income, business income, pensions and rent received by any of these members.
A son or daughter who lives in another house with their own family is not counted.

## 3 What is a land record (khatauni)?
A land record, called khatauni in Nayagaon and Sundarpur, shows who owns a piece of land and how big it is.
In Kaverinadu it is called pahani, and in Thenpuram it is called patta.
You can get a current copy from the tehsil or taluk office, or at a service centre. Farmer schemes need a current land record in your own name.

## 4 What is a UDID card?
UDID means Unique Disability ID. It is a national identity card for persons with disability.
It shows the type of disability and the percentage of disability.
Check the documents section of each scheme guideline to see which disability certificate that scheme asks for.

## 5 Can I apply to more than one scheme?
Yes. You can apply to every scheme for which you meet the conditions.
Each scheme has its own rules, its own documents and its own last date. Being approved for one scheme does not mean you are approved for another.
The SchemeSathi assistant or the service centre operator can help you see which schemes may suit you.

## 6 Is there any fee to apply?
No. There is no fee for any application or for help at a service centre (policy POL-FEE-01).
There is no "release fee" in any scheme. Scheme money is paid only by direct bank transfer into your own account.
If anyone asks you for money, do not pay. Report it to the fraud desk through any service centre or the SchemeSathi assistant.

## 7 How do I check the status of my application?
When you apply, you get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.
Give this number at any service centre, or type it in the SchemeSathi assistant, to see your status.
The target for a decision is 45 days from the date of application. You also get an SMS when a decision is made.

## 8 What if my application is rejected?
The reason for rejection is given in writing.
If you think the decision is wrong, you can file a grievance at any service centre or through the SchemeSathi assistant. It is free.
A grievance must be resolved within 30 working days.
