# Amendment: Revised Income Limit for Grameen Awas Sahayata Yojana
Source: CIR-2026-016 | Issued: 2026-11-10 | Effective from: 2026-11-15

Issued by: Department of Rural Development, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Amendment No.: RD/GAS/2026/064 | Date of issue: 2026-11-10 (10 November 2026) | Scheme: Grameen Awas Sahayata Yojana (SCH-HSG-01)
Amends: Section 3 (Eligibility) of GL-HSG-01

## 1 Background
Section 3 of GL-HSG-01 allows the housing grant only when the annual household income is ₹1,80,000 or less.
Building costs have gone up, and the state government has decided to raise the limit.

## 2 Revised income limit
The income limit in Section 3 of GL-HSG-01 is revised from ₹1,80,000 to ₹2,00,000 with effect from 2026-11-15 (15 November 2026).
The priority condition in Section 3.4 of GL-HSG-01, including the income limit of ₹1,00,000 in clause (b), does not change.
Revised rule tables will be issued separately; until then service centres should refer such cases to the block housing officer.

## 3 Effective date
This amendment takes effect from 2026-11-15 (15 November 2026).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
If an applicant's annual household income is more than ₹1,80,000 but not more than ₹2,00,000, do not reject or turn away the applicant.
Refer the case to the block housing officer until the revised rule tables are issued.
