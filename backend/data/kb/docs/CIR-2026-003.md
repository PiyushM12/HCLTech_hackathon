# Circular: Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti 2026-27 Application Cycle
Source: CIR-2026-003 | Issued: 2026-08-25 | Effective from: 2026-09-01

Issued by: Department of School and Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Circular No.: SHE/KVC/2026/121 | Date of issue: 2026-08-25 (25 August 2026) | Scheme: Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01)

## 1 Background
GL-SCH-01 sets the rules for Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01) for the 2026-27 academic year.
This circular announces the application dates for the 2026-27 cycle.

## 2 Application dates
Applications for the 2026-27 cycle of Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01) open on 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026).
The last date for applications is 2026-11-30 (30 November 2026).

## 3 Effective date
This circular takes effect from 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
Schools and colleges should tell all girl students in Class 9 and above about the scheme.
Service centres should accept applications from 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026) and help students check that their income certificate was issued in the last 12 months.
