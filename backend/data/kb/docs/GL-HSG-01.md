# Grameen Awas Sahayata Yojana – Scheme Guidelines 2026-27
Source: GL-HSG-01 | Issued: 2026-05-20 | Effective from: 2026-06-01

Issued by: Department of Rural Development, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: RD/GAS/2026/027 | Date of issue: 2026-05-20 (20 May 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-HSG-01
Valid until: 2027-03-31 (31 March 2027)

## 1 Purpose
Grameen Awas Sahayata Yojana (SCH-HSG-01) helps poor rural families build a pucca house.
It gives first help to the families who need it most.
These guidelines apply from 2026-06-01 (1 June 2026) to 2027-03-31 (31 March 2027).

## 2 Benefit
An approved household gets a one-time grant of ₹1,20,000 to build a pucca house.
The grant is paid in three stages into the bank account of the applicant:
- Stage 1: when the work starts (foundation),
- Stage 2: when the walls reach roof level,
- Stage 3: when the roof is complete.

The block surveyor checks the work before each stage is paid.

## 3 Eligibility
A household can get the grant only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The household lives in a rural area (a village under a Gram Panchayat).

3.2 The annual household income is ₹1,80,000 or less.

3.3 The total land owned by all household members together is 2.5 acres or less.

3.4 The household also meets the priority condition in Section 3.4 below.

What "household income" means: the total yearly income of all members who live together in the same house.

## 3.4 Priority condition
In addition to the conditions above, the household must meet at least ONE of these:

(a) the household belongs to the Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) category; OR

(b) the annual household income is ₹1,00,000 or less; OR

(c) a member of the household has a disability.

Meeting one of (a), (b) or (c) is enough. A household that meets none of them cannot get the grant in this cycle.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued in the last 12 months |
| Land record of the house plot | Mandatory | Plot must be in the name of a household member |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |
| Caste certificate | Optional | Only if applying under the SC/ST priority condition |
| Photo of the current house | Optional | Taken by the block surveyor if not provided |

## 5 How to apply
1. Visit any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent) with your documents.
2. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
3. The block surveyor visits the house plot to verify the details.
4. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.

## 6 Last date
Applications are accepted from 2026-06-01 (1 June 2026).
The last date for applications is 2027-03-31 (31 March 2027).

## 7 Decision
The Department of Rural Development checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the household can file a grievance.
