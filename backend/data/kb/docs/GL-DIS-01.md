# Divyang Sambal Yojana – Scheme Guidelines
Source: GL-DIS-01 | Issued: 2026-03-20 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Social Welfare, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: SW/DIS/2026/039 | Date of issue: 2026-03-20 (20 March 2026) | Scheme ID: SCH-DIS-01

## 1 Purpose
Divyang Sambal Yojana (SCH-DIS-01) gives monthly support to persons with disability from low-income households.
It also helps them travel freely within the state.
These guidelines apply from 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026).

## 2 Benefit
An approved person gets:
- ₹2,000 per month, paid directly into their bank account; and
- a free bus pass for travel in state transport buses within Mitrapur Pradesh.

## 3 Eligibility
A person can get the benefit only if all of these conditions are met:

3.1 The person has a disability.

3.2 The disability is 40% or more, as certified by the District Medical Board.

3.3 The annual household income is ₹2,00,000 or less.

There is no age limit. For a child, a parent or guardian applies on the child's behalf.

## 4 Documents
| Document | Mandatory or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Disability certificate | Mandatory | Issued by the District Medical Board |
| Income certificate | Mandatory | Issued in the last 12 months |
| Bank passbook | Mandatory | First page with account holder name |
| Passport-size photograph | Optional | Needed for the bus pass |

## 5 How to apply
1. Visit any service centre (Jan Seva Kendra or equivalent). A family member can apply for a person who cannot travel.
2. The operator fills in the form online. There is no fee.
3. You get an acknowledgement slip with an acknowledgement number.
4. The bus pass is given at the same service centre after approval.

## 6 Last date
There is no last date. Applications are open throughout the year.

## 7 Decision
The Department of Social Welfare checks each application and decides on it.
The target is to decide within 45 days of the date of application.
No person can promise or guarantee approval. Only the department decides.
If an application is rejected, the reason is given in writing, and the applicant can file a grievance.
