# Policy on Grievances and Service Timelines
Source: POL-GRV-01 | Issued: 2026-04-01 | Effective from: 2026-04-01

Issued by: Department of Public Services and e-Governance, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Order No.: PSG/POL/2026/003 | Date of issue: 2026-04-01 (1 April 2026) | Applies to: all schemes

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets time limits for answering grievances and for deciding applications.
A grievance can be filed at any service centre or through the SchemeSathi assistant. Filing a grievance is free.

## 2 Grievance timeline
Every grievance must be resolved within 30 working days of the date it is filed.
The citizen gets a grievance number and an SMS when the grievance is resolved.

## 3 Referral pick-up times
When a case is referred to a human officer, the officer must pick it up within these times:
- Urgent referral: within 1 working day.
- High-priority referral: within 2 working days.
- Normal or low priority referral: within 5 working days.

Cases about fraud, demands for money, or a person in distress are treated as urgent.

## 4 Application processing target
The target time for a decision on a submitted application is 45 days from the date of application.
If no decision is made within 45 days, the citizen can file a grievance about the delay.

## 5 Repeated contact
If a citizen contacts the service 3 or more times about the same issue, it is treated as repeated contact.
A repeated-contact case is sent to a human officer for review, even if each answer was correct.
