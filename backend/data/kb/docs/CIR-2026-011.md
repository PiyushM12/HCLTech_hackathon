# Amendment: Last Date Extended for Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti
Source: CIR-2026-011 | Issued: 2026-11-14 | Effective from: 2026-11-15

Issued by: Department of School and Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
Amendment No.: SHE/KVC/2026/139 | Date of issue: 2026-11-14 (14 November 2026) | Scheme: Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01)
Amends: Section 6 (Last date) of GL-SCH-01 | Supersedes: CIR-2026-003

## 1 Background
Section 6 of GL-SCH-01 and circular CIR-2026-003 fixed the last date for applications under Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01) as 2026-11-30 (30 November 2026).
Many schools asked for more time because half-yearly examinations were held in November.

## 2 Extension of last date
The last date for applications under Kanya Vidya Chhatravritti (SCH-SCH-01) is extended from 2026-11-30 (30 November 2026) to 2026-12-15 (15 December 2026).
This amends Section 6 of GL-SCH-01.
This amendment supersedes the last date given in CIR-2026-003. The opening date of 2026-09-01 (1 September 2026) does not change.
All eligibility rules and documents stay the same.

## 3 Effective date
This amendment takes effect from 2026-11-15 (15 November 2026).

## 4 Instructions for service centres
Accept applications under SCH-SCH-01 up to and including 2026-12-15 (15 December 2026).
Tell students and parents who were told the last date is 2026-11-30 that they now have time until 2026-12-15.
